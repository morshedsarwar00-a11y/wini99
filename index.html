FAQ

When are wini99 draws held?

wini99 draws are held on the 1st and 16th of each month, at about 2:30pm Qatar time (AST).

Can residents and visitors in Qatar use this page?

Yes — Qatar is home to a large international community, and this page is written for residents and visitors alike. This page does not sell tickets; to play, use the wini99 app.

Are the prize amounts on this page final?

No — every prize amount here is shown in Qatari Riyal (QR).

How long do I have to claim a prize?

There is nothing to claim from this page.

Is the wini99 app the official lottery app?

No. wini99 is our own number game app — Single Number (predict 1 digit, 9.5x) and Triple Number (predict 3 digits, up to 950x). It does not sell official lottery tickets and is not connected to any official lottery body.
